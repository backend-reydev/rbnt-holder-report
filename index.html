At a glance

The quarter in six points

Staking grew

The two Reddex pools held 754.8 million RBNT on 30 September, against 639.1 million on 30 June (+18.1%). Most of the change was in the no lock pool (+82.6 million). Addresses with an open stake went from 1,156 to 1,140 in the no lock pool and from 223 to 217 in the 365 day pool.

Reserved wallets mostly moved RBNT between themselves

Ecosystem (Locked) sent 500 million to Ecosystem 2 (Unlocked); Ecosystem 2 (Unlocked) sent 253.1 million to Investors; Governance (Locked) sent 33.3 million to Governance (Unlocked). Only Ecosystem (Unlocked) sent RBNT outside the reserved group: 43.8 million RBNT, mostly into WRBNT and the two staking pools.

Vesting contracts paid out

Contracts whose verified code is a vesting manager held 1.68 billion RBNT (as WRBNT) on 30 June and 1.58 billion on 30 September. They sent 127.8 million WRBNT to wallets in 1,885 transfers during the quarter.

The largest wallets added on balance

The 100 wallets that were largest on 30 June held 515 million RBNT then and 540.9 million on 30 September, counting wrapped and staked RBNT. 39 of them added more than 1%, 45 stayed within 1% and 6 reduced by more than 1%.

Holdings stayed concentrated

The largest 100 wallets held 76.7% of all wallet-held RBNT at the end, against 75.8% at the start. Half of it sits in just 12 wallets. The middle wallet holds 5.84 RBNT.

A few more wallets hold RBNT

53,575 wallets held 1 RBNT or more on 30 September, 157 more than on 30 June. 243 wallets crossed 1 RBNT during the quarter and 86 dropped below it.

Read this first. This report describes what the public Redbelly chain shows. It is about trends, not people. It names only addresses that are already public: Redbelly's nine reserved wallets from the DAO treasury page, the Wrapped RBNT contract and the two Reddex staking pools. The explorer labels no exchange wallet on Redbelly, so none is named. Every other address appears only inside a group of at least five.

The chain records that coins moved. It does not record why, or who controls an address. When a group's holdings fall, the coins left those addresses: that may be a sale, a move to another address of the same owner, or a deposit into a contract. This report makes no price forecast and gives no advice.

New to this? Three ideas the report uses

Address. Every coin sits at an address, a public account number anyone can look up. Wallet or contract. A wallet address is controlled by a private key held by a person or organisation. A contract address is a program that holds coins under its own fixed rules. Counted once. Wrapped RBNT (WRBNT) and staked RBNT still belong to whoever wrapped or staked them, so this report counts them with that holder and never a second time at the contract.

Section 1

Who holds the RBNT

On 30 September 59.3% of all RBNT sat in Redbelly's reserved wallets, 19.6% with other wallets and 21.1% in contracts other than the named ones. The largest group of contracts is vesting contracts.

All 10 billion RBNT were created when the network started. Figure 1 adds up every holding on both dates and groups it by the kind of address that owns it. Wrapped and staked RBNT are counted with the address that wrapped or staked them.

Contracts publish their code on the explorer when their authors verify it. The name in that code says what kind of program a contract is, such as a vesting contract or a multi-signature wallet, though not who controls it. This report uses those names only to group contracts, and only for kinds with at least five contracts. Vesting contracts hold coins and release them to set addresses over time. Multi-signature wallets need more than one key to move coins. Bridge contracts hold RBNT moved to or from other blockchains. Trading pools hold the coins people trade against on decentralised exchanges.

Where the coins physically sit

The view above counts wrapped and staked RBNT with its owner. Figure 2 shows the same supply by the address that physically holds it, so the WRBNT contract and the two staking pools appear on their own.

How complete is this?

The balances read add up to 99.9996% of the 10 billion supply on 30 September. The remaining 36,485 RBNT sit at addresses that never sent or received a transaction this scan could see, such as addresses funded when the network started. That amount changed by 11 RBNT in the quarter, so it cannot change any trend here.

Where RBNT moved during the quarter

Figure 3 follows every transfer of RBNT and WRBNT between groups from 1 July to 30 September. The largest flows were stakes into the Reddex pools (196.4 million from wallets), WRBNT released by vesting contracts to wallets (127.8 million), RBNT unwrapped from the WRBNT contract (115.9 million to wallets) and withdrawals and rewards from the pools (95.7 million). Transfers inside the same group, such as from one reserved wallet to another, are left out of the diagram and shown in the table.

Section 2

How many holders, and how big

53,575 wallets held 1 RBNT or more on 30 September, 157 more than on 30 June. Most hold a little: the most common size is 1 to 100 RBNT.

A holding here is everything a wallet owns: RBNT in the wallet, plus WRBNT, plus RBNT it has staked. Wallets with less than 1 RBNT are left out of the counts, because many addresses only keep a tiny amount left over from fees (111,509 such wallets on 30 September). Named wallets and contracts are not in these counts either; they are shown in section 1.

Figure 4 shows the same pattern from both sides. 89% of wallets hold between 1 and 100 RBNT, yet together they hold 0.03% of wallet RBNT. The 23 wallets with 10 million and over RBNT hold 60%. The table under the figure also shows the two month ends inside the quarter.

Section 3

How concentrated holdings are

The largest 10 wallets held 48.4% of all wallet-held RBNT on 30 September and the largest 100 held 76.7%. Both shares rose slightly over the quarter.

Concentration asks how much of the RBNT sits with a few holders. It is measured here over wallets only, because Redbelly's reserved wallets and contracts are large by design and would hide what holders are doing. Three measures are used: the share held by the largest wallets, the number of wallets that together hold half, and the Gini score, which runs from 0 (every wallet holds the same) to 1 (one wallet holds everything).

Why this matters, and what it does not mean

A concentrated spread means a small number of wallets could move a large share of the supply. It does not say who those wallets belong to: one wallet can be shared by many people, such as an exchange, and one person can use many wallets.

Section 4

Wallets that started the quarter large mostly held steady or added. The 21 wallets that started with 10 million and over RBNT held 1.06 billion then and 1.09 billion at the end.

To see accumulation and selling without following any single wallet, wallets are put into groups by their holding on 30 June and the same wallets are followed to 30 September. A wallet added if its holding rose by more than 1%, reduced if it fell by more than 1%, and stayed within 1% otherwise. Staking or wrapping does not count as reducing, because the holding includes wrapped and staked RBNT.

Section 5

Staking in the Reddex pools

The two Reddex pools held 754.8 million RBNT on 30 September, up from 639.1 million on 30 June (+18.1%).

Staking means locking RBNT in a pool to earn rewards. Reddex runs two RBNT pools: one with no lock, where stakers can take their RBNT out at any time, and one with a 365 day lock, where each stake can only be taken out a year after it was made. As the brief asks, the headline figures are each pool's RBNT balance at the start and end of the quarter.

A pool's balance includes reward funds as well as stakes. Rewards are paid in RBNT out of the pool's own balance. So the balance is the stakers' RBNT plus RBNT set aside for rewards. The pool contract also records the total staked by its stakers (its totalStake), which lets this report split the two: staked RBNT, and reward funds = balance minus totalStake.

When locked RBNT unlocks

Each stake in the 365 day pool unlocks one year after it was made. Figure 13 adds up, by month, the RBNT that was still staked on 30 September and when it becomes free to withdraw. Unlocking does not mean the RBNT will be withdrawn; stakers can leave it in the pool. 74.9 million RBNT unlocks in the next three months. Another 94.8 million RBNT was still staked on 30 September although its lock had already ended.

Wrapped RBNT

WRBNT is RBNT wrapped into a token so it can be used by apps that expect one. The WRBNT contract holds one RBNT for every WRBNT. The supply fell from 1.82 billion to 1.74 billion over the quarter.

Section 7

How this report was made

Every balance was read directly from the Redbelly chain at two fixed blocks, and every published figure was read again from the node by a separate check before release.

  • Two fixed moments. The start is block 3,025,809, the last block before midnight UTC at the end of 30 June 2026. The end is block 3,209,414, the last block before midnight UTC at the end of 30 September. Month ends and each day of the quarter use the same rule.
  • Every address. All 3,209,415 blocks from the network's start to the end block were read, along with 1,753,030 internal transfers from the explorer, to find every address that could hold RBNT: 761,594 addresses in total.
  • Balances from the chain. Each address's RBNT, WRBNT and staked RBNT were read at each snapshot block. Balances shown by the explorer's lists are never used, because they are today's figures and rounded.
  • Counted once. RBNT inside the WRBNT contract and staked RBNT inside the pools are counted with their owners. The part of a pool's balance above its totalStake is reported as that pool's reward funds.
  • Groups, never individuals. Only publicly named wallets are named. Every other address is counted only in a group of at least five, and a scan of every published file fails the build if any other address appears.
  • Exact checks. 58 checks must pass in wei before anything is written: WRBNT supply equals the sum of all WRBNT balances, stakers add up to each pool's totalStake, stakes minus withdrawals equal the change in totalStake, every group adds up to the RBNT located, and every named wallet reconciles to the last unit.

Limits

  • The chain shows that coins moved, not who controls an address or why. A fall in a group's holdings may be a sale, a move between a holder's own addresses or a deposit into a contract.
  • One address is not one person. Counts of wallets show how widely RBNT is spread across addresses.
  • Internal transfers come from the explorer, because the public node has no trace interface. They are checked indirectly: every named wallet, including the WRBNT contract and both pools, reconciles exactly.
  • The explorer's address list was read for its 20,000 largest addresses. A public label on a smaller address would not be seen.

Words used

Words used in this report

RBNT
The coin of the Redbelly network. There are 10 billion in total, all created when the network started.
Address
A public account on the chain. Anyone can look up its balance.
Wallet
An address controlled by a private key, held by a person or an organisation.
Contract
An address that is a program. It holds and moves coins under its own fixed rules.
WRBNT
Wrapped RBNT. A token backed one to one by RBNT held in the WRBNT contract.
Staking
Locking RBNT in a pool to earn rewards, paid in RBNT.
totalStake
The pool contract's own record of how much RBNT its stakers have staked.
Reward funds
RBNT in a pool above its totalStake, used to pay rewards.
Reserved wallets
Redbelly's wallets listed on the DAO treasury page: team, investors, reserve, ecosystem, governance and USYD/CSIRO.
Snapshot block
The block whose balances stand for a moment in time: the last block before midnight UTC.
Gini score
A single number for concentration: 0 means every wallet holds the same, 1 means one wallet holds all.
Lorenz curve
A chart of how much the smallest holders hold together. A straight line would mean equal holdings.
Wei
The smallest unit of RBNT. 1 RBNT is 1018 wei. All checks in this report are exact to the wei.
