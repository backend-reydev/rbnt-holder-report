What this report covers

Distribution

How RBNT is spread across holders, in balance bands, and how that changed over the quarter.

Largest holders

Accumulation and selling among the top 10, 50, 100 and 500 wallets, shown as groups only.

Staking

RBNT held in the Reddex no lock and 365 day pools at the start and end of the quarter.

Named wallets

Flows in and out of Redbelly's reserved wallets and explorer labelled exchanges.

Method and data

The report names only wallets that are already public. Every other wallet appears only inside a group. Wrapped RBNT is counted once. Each number is logged with its source and the date it was fetched.
