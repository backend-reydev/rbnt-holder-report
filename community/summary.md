# RBNT holders in Q3 2026: community summary

The first quarterly on-chain holder report covers 1 July to 30 September 2026. Every number is read from the Redbelly chain at block 3,025,809 (30 June, 23:57 UTC) and block 3,209,414 (30 September, 23:57 UTC). It describes trends in groups and names only wallets that are already public.

**Staking.** The two Reddex RBNT pools held 754.8 million RBNT at the end of the quarter, against 639.1 million at the start (+18.1%). Most of the change was in the no lock pool. A pool's balance includes RBNT set aside for rewards as well as stakes; the report shows both parts.

**Holders.** 53,575 wallets held 1 RBNT or more on 30 September, against 53,418 on 30 June. Holdings stayed concentrated: the largest 100 wallets held 76.7% of wallet-held RBNT (75.8% at the start).

**Largest holders.** The 100 wallets that were largest on 30 June held 515 million RBNT then and 540.9 million at the end, counting wrapped and staked RBNT. 39 added more than 1%, 45 held within 1% and 6 reduced by more than 1%.

**Reserved wallets.** Redbelly's nine reserved wallets held 5.93 billion RBNT at the end, against 5.97 billion at the start. Most movement was between reserved wallets: Ecosystem (Locked) sent 500 million to Ecosystem 2 (Unlocked); Ecosystem 2 (Unlocked) sent 253.1 million to Investors; Governance (Locked) sent 33.3 million to Governance (Unlocked). Only Ecosystem (Unlocked) (43.8 million RBNT) sent RBNT outside the group, mostly into WRBNT and the two staking pools.

**Vesting contracts.** Contracts whose verified code is a vesting manager sent 127.8 million WRBNT to wallets during the quarter. They held 1.68 billion at the start and 1.58 billion at the end.

**Wrapped RBNT.** WRBNT supply went from 1.82 billion to 1.74 billion, matched exactly by the RBNT backing it.

The chain shows that coins moved, not who controls an address or why. This summary makes no price forecast and gives no advice.

The full report, its method and every data file are published with this summary.
