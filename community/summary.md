# RBNT holders in Q3 2026: the short version

This is the first quarterly look at how RBNT holders behaved, covering 1 July to 30 September 2026. We read every figure from the Redbelly chain, comparing block 3,025,809 (end of 30 June) with block 3,209,414 (end of 30 September). We report groups, never individual holders, and we name only wallets that are already public.

**Staking grew.** The two Reddex pools held 754.8 million RBNT at the end of September, against 639.1 million at the end of June (+18.1%), with the no lock pool accounting for the larger part. Part of every pool's balance is a reserve for paying rewards; the full report separates it from the stakes.

**Vesting contracts paid out.** Contracts running token vesting code sent 127.8 million WRBNT to wallets during the quarter.

**The biggest wallets added.** The 100 largest unnamed wallets on 30 June held 1.38 billion RBNT (wrapped and staked included) and 1.42 billion three months later. 43 grew by more than 1%, 49 stayed within 1% and 8 shrank.

**Still top-heavy.** 53,575 wallets held 1 RBNT or more (53,418 in June). The ten largest hold 48.4% of what unnamed wallets hold.

**Reserved wallets.** Between them the nine reserved wallets went from 5.97 billion RBNT to 5.93 billion. Most of what they moved stayed inside the group; only Ecosystem (Unlocked) sent coins outside it (43.8 million RBNT), mostly into WRBNT and the pools' reward reserves.

**Wrapped RBNT.** WRBNT in circulation went from 1.82 billion to 1.74 billion, backed one to one throughout.

The chain shows coins moving, not who moved them or why. Nothing here is a forecast or advice.

The full report, its method and every data file are published alongside this summary.
