Method and data sources

This note explains how the RBNT Holder Report is produced, so that anyone can check this edition or produce the next one. It covers what is measured, where every number comes from, the checks that must pass, and the exact steps to repeat the work.

Edition 1 covers Q3 2026, 1 July to 30 September 2026.

1. What the report measures

Question in the brief How it is measured Files
How holdings are spread, and how that changed Every wallet's holding at the quarter start, both month ends and the quarter end, in size bands; share of the largest 10, 100 and 1,000 wallets; the number of wallets that together hold half; Gini score; Lorenz curve 04, 05, 05b
Accumulation and selling among the largest holders, in groups Wallets grouped by their holding at the start and followed to the end: how many added more than 1%, stayed within 1%, reduced by more than 1% or fell below 1 RBNT; net change per group; the same for rank groups (largest 10, 11 to 100, 101 to 1,000); how each group holds its RBNT (in the wallet, wrapped, staked); a band migration matrix 06, 06b, 06c

2. Definitions

  • Snapshot. The snapshot for a moment T is the highest block whose timestamp is earlier than T. The start is the last block before 1 July 2026 00:00 UTC and the end is the last block before 1 October 2026 00:00 UTC, so the end includes the whole of 30 September.

End of 31 August 2026 3,161,410 2026-08-31 23:57:49
End, end of 30 September 2026 3,209,414 2026-09-30 23:57:35

Each day of the quarter and every seventh day also have a snapshot by the same rule (93 daily snapshots), used for the pools, WRBNT and the reserved wallets. * Holding of an address = native RBNT + WRBNT balance + RBNT staked in either Reddex pool (userInfo(address).amount). Wrapping or staking is therefore never counted as selling. * Counted once. RBNT inside the WRBNT contract backs WRBNT one to one, and the staked RBNT inside each pool belongs to its stakers. Both are counted with their owners and never again at the contract. The part of a pool's balance above its totalStake is that pool's reward funds. Any RBNT in the WRBNT contract above the WRBNT supply would be shown as "WRBNT contract surplus"; in this edition there is none. * Named addresses are the nine reserved wallets on the DAO treasury page, the WRBNT contract, the two Reddex RBNT pools and any address the explorer gives a public name. They are reported by name and address. * Wallet = an address with no code at the end block. An address carrying only an EIP-7702 delegation marker (0xef0100...) is still a key-held wallet. Contract = an address with code. * Contract kinds come from the contract name in the verified source on the explorer (for a proxy, the name of its implementation). Rules, applied in order to the lower case name: vest vesting contracts; safe, multisig, gnosis multi-signature wallets; bridge, oft, portal, gateway, teleport bridge contracts; pair, pool, swap, router, amm, algebra, liquidity trading pools; stak, farm, reward other staking contracts; no verified source: unverified contracts; anything else: other verified contracts. A kind with fewer than five contracts is folded into "Other contracts". The rules are in config/q3-2026.json. * Holder counts, bands and concentration use wallets holding at least 1 RBNT. Named wallets and contracts are left out, because the reserved wallets are large by design and one contract can hold coins for many people. They are shown separately in section 1 of the report. * Bands (lower bounds, RBNT): 1, 100, 1,000, 10,000, 100,000, 1 million, 10 million, 100 million. A band with fewer than five wallets at any of the four main snapshots is merged into the band below it. * Cohorts are fixed by holding at the start and followed to the end. "Added" means more than 1% higher, "reduced" more than 1% lower, "within 1%" otherwise, "below 1 at end" means the wallet ended under 1 RBNT.

Pool balances include reward funds

Both pools run the verified contract ReddexRBNTLockStaker. stake() adds the RBNT sent to the caller's userInfo.amount and to totalStake; unstake() subtracts it and sends it back; rewards are paid in RBNT from the pool's own balance. A pool's RBNT balance is therefore stakes plus reward funds. As the brief asks, the headline staking figures are the pools' RBNT balances at the start and end. The report then splits each balance into staked RBNT (totalStake) and reward funds (balance minus totalStake).

Network fees and governor addresses

Redbelly blocks name the zero address as their miner, yet fees are not destroyed: the network credits them to addresses listed in block headers (the governor set, in extraData) as balance changes without a transaction. Those addresses are collected from every block header so their balances are read too. This is also why every balance is read directly at each snapshot instead of being rebuilt by adding up transfers.

3. Privacy rules applied

  • Only named addresses are ever shown with a name or an address.
  • Every other address is counted only inside a group of at least five. Size bands, stake size bands, contract kinds and lock calendar months with fewer than five members are merged or shown as "<5".
  • Contract kinds come from the contract's own verified code, which describes the program, not who controls it. No owner is inferred.
  • No label is guessed. The explorer labelled no exchange wallet in this edition, so no address is treated as an exchange.
  • The local cache that holds per-address data (cache/) is never published. scripts/10_verify.py scans every published file and fails if any address outside the named list appears, and the same scan runs in the unit tests.

5. Steps

Each step is a script in scripts/ and keeps its progress in cache/<edition>.sqlite, so it can be stopped and restarted.

  1. 01_snapshots.py finds every snapshot block (start, end, month ends, weekly, daily) by binary search on block timestamps from the node, checks that the next block is at or after the boundary, and cross-checks each with the explorer's getblocknobytime. The explorer counts a block stamped exactly at the boundary as "before" it; that is the only difference allowed.
  2. 02_scan_chain.py reads eth_getBlockReceipts and the header of every block from 0 to the end block, in batches of up to 100 blocks (a batch the node refuses as too large is split until it fits). It keeps every sender, recipient and created contract, the logs of WRBNT and the two pools, the 20-byte addresses in each header's extraData, and every transaction inside the quarter with its value and fee.
  3. 03_internal_transfers.py pages through the explorer's txlistinternal by block range from block 0 to the end block. A range that returns the 10,000 row limit is split in half until complete. Every party to a successful internal transfer with value, and every contract created internally, joins the address list; inside the quarter the full rows are kept.
  4. 04_explorer_labels.py reads the explorer's address list, largest first, for public name tags, and the WRBNT holder list.
  5. 05_balances.py reads, at the snapshot blocks: native RBNT of every address at the start and end, and at both month ends for every address that was non-zero at either end or active in the quarter (any other address cannot have changed); native RBNT of each named wallet daily; WRBNT balanceOf of every address that ever touched WRBNT and totalSupply; userInfo in both pools for every address that ever staked, and each pool's totalStake and balance at every snapshot; getStakeUnlockTimes for every open stake at the end; and eth_getCode at the end block for every address that held anything.
  6. 06_contracts.py reads the verified contract name of every contract that held at least 1 RBNT (counting WRBNT and stakes) at a main snapshot.
  7. 07_analyse.py builds every table in data/processed/q3-2026/ and figures.json, running the exact checks below first. It stops if any fails.
  8. 08_charts.py draws every chart as SVG from the CSV files.
  9. 09_build.py writes the web page, the PDF (printed from the same page), the Word file (from the same content), the workbook, the community summary, the X thread and the social cards. Every number in the text is read from figures.json or a CSV, never typed by hand; sentences that describe a direction pick their verb from the numbers.
  10. 10_verify.py re-reads every named wallet balance, every pool's totalStake and the WRBNT supply straight from the node, re-reads 600 random cached balances, re-checks the snapshot boundaries, scans every published file for unnamed addresses, dashes, emoji, price talk and credit lines, and checks every X post fits in 280 characters. Results go to verification.json.

Checks that must pass

All are exact equalities in wei (1 RBNT = 10^18 wei), at every main snapshot unless stated.

  • WRBNT totalSupply equals the sum of every WRBNT balance, and the WRBNT contract holds at least that much RBNT.
  • For each pool, the stakers' userInfo amounts add up to totalStake, and the pool's balance covers totalStake.
  • For each pool over the quarter, RBNT in Stake records minus RBNT in Unstake records equals the change in totalStake.
  • The lock calendar of the 365 day pool adds up to its totalStake at the end.
  • Holdings (with WRBNT and stakes moved to their owners and reward funds left with the pools) add up to the native RBNT read, at every snapshot.
  • Group changes add up to the change in RBNT located.
  • Every reported contract kind has at least five members.
  • Every named wallet reconciles: start balance + RBNT in - RBNT out - fees = end balance, to the wei.
  • The balances read cover more than 99.99% of the 10 billion supply and never exceed it.

Completeness

All 10 billion RBNT existed from the network's first block. The balances read cover the share of supply stated in 01_supply_check.csv at each snapshot. The remainder sits at addresses that never appear in any transaction, internal transfer or block header the scan could see, such as addresses funded at genesis that never moved. It changes by only a few RBNT over the quarter, so it cannot change any trend.

6. How to produce the next edition

  1. Install Python 3.11 or later and pip install -r requirements.txt. Chromium is needed for the PDF and the chart PNGs (any Playwright install).
  2. Copy config/q3-2026.json to config/q4-2026.json. Change edition, period and main_snapshots (month end names follow month_YYYY-MM-01), and set snapshot_blocks to null.
  3. Re-read the DAO treasury page. If a wallet was added or changed, update reserved_wallets and the date in its source.
  4. Run make data analyse build verify EDITION=q4-2026. A run from an empty cache reads about 3.3 million blocks from public endpoints. Busy stretches of the chain are split automatically.
  5. Read the report end to end. Text that describes a direction is generated from the numbers, but read each sentence against the new figures.
  6. If the explorer has started labelling exchange wallets, they appear in figures.json under labelled_exchanges and are added to the named list automatically; extend section 6 with their flows.
  7. Run make check and publish only when verify ends with no failures.

7. Limits

  • The chain shows that coins moved, not who controls an address or why. A fall in a group's holdings may be a sale, a move between a holder's own addresses, or a deposit into a contract.
  • One address is not one person. Counts of wallets show how widely RBNT is spread across addresses.
  • Internal transfers come from the explorer because the public node has no trace interface. They are checked indirectly: every named wallet, including the WRBNT contract and both pools, which move RBNT almost only through internal transfers, reconciles to the wei.
  • The explorer's address list was read for its 20,000 largest addresses. A public label on a smaller address would not be seen.
